      Mouse and touch

      • Orbit: drag with the left mouse button or one finger.
      • Pan: right-drag, Shift+drag or two fingers.
      • Zoom: mouse wheel, pinch, or the + / − buttons.
      • Focus: double-click the model to fly there. Click a gold marker for information.
