# Research links - Cologne Cathedral

Every link below was checked automatically on **2026-11-18** with a desktop browser User-Agent. `200` means it loaded. Other codes are listed honestly; most are bot blocks that open normally in a browser. YouTube links were checked through YouTube oEmbed, which also returns 404 when the uploader disables embedding.

> **Licence rule for models:** you may *look at* anything here. You may only *ship* (embed, bundle or redistribute) material whose licence allows it - the files in `references/` (see `references/ATTRIBUTION.md`) and procedural geometry you generate yourself. Do not copy images, plans or models from koelner-dom.de, Google, YouTube, WDR or Sketchfab non-commercial items into the app.

## How to use these links during a build

1. These links are your research: use them both for facts (heights, levels, artworks, history; the official site and Wikipedia are the most reliable) and for *appearance*. There is no prescribed specification; build your own interpretation.
2. For the exterior, start with E01 (Riss F), X01, X03, X05, X17 and X21 in `references/`, then the drone videos.
3. For the interior, start with I01, I04, I06, I26 and the Google Arts & Culture Street View.
4. For hidden levels (roof truss, bell frame, excavations), use WDR Dom 360 plus I17-I21.
5. For the true footprint, compare your plan with OpenStreetMap way 4532022 and P01/P02.
